Privacy notice

How the diagnostic handles data

Last updated: August 4, 2026.

What is collected

The diagnostic asks for process answers, name, work email, company and approximate monthly lead volume. The calculated result and answers are stored locally in your browser. When you choose to request a review, your email application opens with a prepared message so you can decide whether to send it.

Current technical behaviour

The current static version does not send diagnostic data to a server automatically because no production webhook is configured. A future version may use a clearly identified form or CRM provider; this notice must be updated before that happens.

Purpose

Information you intentionally send is used to respond to your request, provide a process review and discuss a possible workshop or implementation service. It is not sold.

Partner relationships

Referral Growth Engine may receive compensation when you choose a recommended platform through a disclosed partner link. Diagnostic answers are not shared with a partner platform merely because you complete the diagnostic.

Retention and deletion

Local diagnostic data can be removed by clearing this site’s browser storage. Information sent by email may be retained for correspondence and legitimate business records. You may request deletion by emailing safal0645@protonmail.com, subject to legal record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You may use the educational content without sending the diagnostic result. You may opt out of future direct communications at any time.

Security

Do not send passwords, payment card data, tax identifiers or sensitive personal information through the diagnostic or email review request.
