# Cohort 01 — 30 Agency Validation Accounts

Research date: 2026-08-04

## Purpose

Validate whether a problem-first, QA-led Revenue Leak Score creates qualified conversations more effectively than a software-first affiliate pitch.

## Rules

- Use only the public business channel recorded in `data/referral-growth/lead-pipeline.csv`.
- Send one personalised permission-based message; no bulk blast.
- Do not send a partner link before programme approval, disclosure placement and attribution testing.
- Stop immediately after an opt-out.
- Treat every observation below as a testable hypothesis, not proof that the company has a broken funnel.
