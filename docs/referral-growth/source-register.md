## Change-control rule

When a term changes:

1. update `program-scorecard.csv`;
2. record old and new terms with effective/observed date;
3. update landing-page and launch claims;
4. recalculate unit economics;
5. check whether current referrals are grandfathered;
6. rerun attribution and payout test cases;
7. notify affected prospects when the change materially alters an active offer.
