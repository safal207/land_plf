# Outreach and Sales Scripts

The goal is a qualified diagnostic conversation, not mass link distribution.

## Personalisation variables

Before contacting an account, collect only publicly relevant business data:

- company and niche;
- observed lead entry point;
- visible booking path;
- likely CRM or form tool when publicly detectable;
- one specific friction or unanswered question;
- source URL and observation date.

Do not pretend to know private conversion data.

# Discovery call — 30 minutes

## 0–3 minutes: contract

> We’ll map one revenue path, identify the highest-confidence bottleneck and decide whether a paid workshop makes sense. I won’t recommend changing platforms until we know the current system cannot support the required process.

## 3–12 minutes: current state

- Where do leads originate?
- Which system creates the first record?
- Who owns a new lead and how is that proved?
- What is the actual response-time distribution?
- What happens after no reply?
- How are meetings booked, confirmed and recovered after no-show?
- What event means "qualified", "offered" and "won"?
- Which source proves payment?
- Can campaign, CRM and payment records be reconciled?

## 12–20 minutes: impact and evidence

- How often does the failure occur?
- What evidence supports that estimate?
- What is the approximate value of one recovered qualified opportunity?
- What have you already tried?
- What prevents the team from fixing it now?

## 20–25 minutes: target state

> When `[event A]` happens, `[owner/system]` must produce `[event B]` within `[time]`. If it fails, `[recovery]` occurs. The evidence lives in `[source]`.

## 25–30 minutes: next step

Offer the Revenue Trace Workshop when:

- the problem is recognised;
- data access is possible;
- the buyer owns the process;
- the expected value justifies the work;
- there is no request for deceptive or illegal outreach.

# Objection handling

## "Send me the affiliate link"

> I’ll send a link after we confirm the platform fits the process. Otherwise you may buy a tool that does not solve the bottleneck, and neither of us benefits from that.

## "Can you guarantee revenue?"

> No. I can guarantee the agreed mapping, implementation and QA work. Revenue depends on lead quality, offer, sales execution, market conditions and customer behaviour.

## "We need more leads, not operations"

> That may be true. First, let’s inspect a sample of recent lost leads. If the transitions are reliable and the main constraint is genuinely traffic, I’ll say so.

## "We already automated this"

> Great. Then the test is whether failures are observable and recoverable and whether the CRM, campaign and payment records reconcile. Existing automation is evidence to inspect, not a reason to replace it.
