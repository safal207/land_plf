## Brand profile

**Brand / business name:** Referral Growth Engine by Alex Lim  
**Website:** use the deployed diagnostic URL after merge

**Core expertise:** software QA, systems analysis, API and process testing, AI automation, product marketing and PLF launches

## Short company description

Referral Growth Engine helps small agencies and education businesses identify revenue leaks between lead capture and payment. We use a QA-led diagnostic, educational launch content and implementation sprints to improve response time, follow-up, booking, no-show recovery and attribution. When a partner platform is the right fit, we recommend it transparently and help the customer activate it successfully.

## Promotion methods

- original educational articles and videos;
- Product Launch Formula prelaunch content;
- free Revenue Leak Score diagnostic;
- live workshops and product demonstrations;
- email to opted-in subscribers;
- LinkedIn, Telegram and YouTube content;
- personalised, low-volume B2B outreach;
- customer onboarding and implementation support.

We will not use misleading claims, fake scarcity, cookie stuffing, self-referrals, trademark bidding or unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Why the audience is relevant

Our audience has operational problems that the recommended platforms are designed to solve: disconnected lead sources, weak follow-up, booking friction, poor attribution and difficulty launching or monetising educational products. The content begins with the business problem and measurement plan, not with a software pitch.

## How success will be measured

- qualified diagnostic completions;
- activated trials rather than raw clicks;
- paid conversion;
- customer retention at 30, 90 and 180 days;
- implementation outcomes;
- attribution accuracy and commission reconciliation;
- complaint, unsubscribe and refund rates.
